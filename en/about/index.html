Engineering at Afzar Azma

Afzar Azma designs and manufactures metering equipment, from smart electricity meters to the devices that connect their data to the network.

A journey that began in 1980

Electronic Afzar Azma was established in 1980 by graduates of the electrical engineering faculty at Sharif University of Technology. Educational, laboratory and telecommunications equipment form part of the company’s engineering background.

Metering at the centre of our work

Afzar Azma entered electricity meter manufacturing in 2001. Today, its published range includes single-phase and three-phase smart meters and communication equipment.

From design through production

The company’s work spans mechanical and electronic design, manufacturing, assembly and product testing. Its head office is in Tehran and its factory is in Hashtgerd Industrial City.
