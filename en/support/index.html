Frequently asked questions

How do I request a warranty assessment?

Contact the company with your device documents. Coverage and duration depend on the product and purchase documents; the service team confirms eligibility.

Where can I find a reading or installation guide?

Use the model-specific manuals below or on each product page. If your model is not listed, send its name and serial number to the service team.

What is required for a direct purchase?

Direct meter purchases require a valid introduction letter from an electricity distribution or regional electricity company. Contact the company to coordinate an order.

Service and warranty terms

Report a fault by telephone first, then send written supporting details and clear photographs within 48 hours. Coverage and duration are assessed against the device’s warranty documents.

  • Non-standard installation, unauthorised opening or third-party repair may invalidate coverage.
  • The serial number and device seals must remain intact.
  • Damage from voltage fluctuations, natural events, environmental corrosion and impact is excluded under the published warranty conditions.

Coordinate device returns with the service team. The buyer is responsible for shipping costs for a faulty product.

Complaints and service follow-up
